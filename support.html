Getting started

Screen Ruler requires macOS 14 or later. Choose Playground to try the tools without screen permission, or New measurement to measure other apps. The default global shortcut is ⌘ ⌥ R; change it in Settings → Shortcuts.

Auto measures along the pointer's row and column; click to keep the preview. Drag with Box to measure a rectangle, or with Distance to measure between two points. Click with H Guide or V Guide to place a reference line. Open Details for exact values, detection controls, and your measurement list.

Screen Ruler stays in the menu bar while running. Its Dock icon appears while the workspace, Settings, or another regular window is open, and stays available for minimized or hidden windows. When only the measuring panels remain, the Dock icon disappears. Closing a window does not end a measurement.

Use the menu-bar commands New measurement, Open Screen Ruler, and Settings… to get back to work. The global shortcut also remains available. Choose Done to end the current session, or Quit Screen Ruler to exit the app.

Screen access is missing

Open Settings → Privacy → Screen Recording settings in Screen Ruler. In macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security, enable Screen Ruler under Screen Recording or Screen & System Audio Recording. The category name depends on your macOS version; the app does not capture audio.

If macOS asks you to quit and reopen the app, do so, then start a new measurement. Check that you are opening the same installed copy you granted permission to. Playground remains available without access.

If access still appears unavailable after replacing or rebuilding a local app, quit Screen Ruler, close and reopen System Settings, remove the stale entry, and add the exact app you use. macOS may ask you to authenticate. Locally rebuilt apps need a stable signing identity for permission to persist across rebuilds.

Protected video or other restricted content can appear blank even when permission is granted. Screen Ruler cannot measure hidden or protected pixels.

Auto measures the wrong boundary

Auto detects continuous color regions along two lines through your pointer. It does not read an app's source layout or identify semantic objects. Text, shadows, gradients, and small color changes can interrupt the scan.

  • Move the pointer onto a uniform part of the object you want to measure.
  • Adjust Tolerance in Details. A lower value stops at smaller color changes; a higher value can extend through similar colors.
  • Choose Horizontal, Vertical, or Both to control the axes being measured.
  • Press Space to freeze the screen for editing. Use Box or Distance when automatic detection cannot isolate the desired boundary.
  • Use the pixel loupe to inspect edges, and hold Option to temporarily suspend snapping.

A saved Auto crosshair passes through the pointer position used to record it. Select a measurement to edit its values, drag its handles, or Command-drag it to move. Arrow keys nudge by one selected unit; Shift nudges by ten.

Multiple displays, pixels, and points

A new live session covers all connected displays by default. Move the pointer between displays to bring the tools and Details panel with you. Each measurement belongs to its original display, and a single drag stays on the display where it started. Choose display lets you start a session on one display instead.

Playground opens only on the display containing the workspace or floating tools panel. The other displays stay available.

Pixels refer to captured image pixels. Points refer to macOS logical coordinates. On a 2× capture, a 100-point span is 200 captured pixels. Display scaling can affect this relationship; Screen Ruler does not infer CSS units or the physical panel's pixel dimensions. Details shows the current capture scale.

If a display is disconnected or its capture resolution changes, follow the app's message and start a new session. Previously recorded measurements retain their original display information.

What does pinning do?

Pin keeps a recorded measurement visible as a fixed reference while you interact with other apps. Click the pin icon on a measurement card in Details, or press P while the measuring canvas has focus. Pins do not track a window or follow a moving object.

Interact lets clicks pass through to other apps and shows only pinned references. Return restores your previous measuring mode. Interact entered from Live continues to read screen pixels; entering it from Frozen keeps capture paused. Choose Done to stop the session and release captured images.

Saved sessions and exports

Use Save or ⌘ S to keep measurements and notes in your local library. Finishing, starting another session, opening another saved session, or quitting also preserves the current measurements. Every image you export or copy is kept in the session's image log. Open a session to see its thumbnails, view an image, or copy or save the exact PNG again. Full-screen captures are discarded when the session ends.

Each measurement has its own trash button. Use Undo in Details or press ⌘ Z with the measuring canvas focused to undo measurement changes. While editing notes or a title, Undo applies to that text. Delete a saved session using the trash button beside its name in Your sessions, then confirm. This removes the session's measurements, notes, and local image copies. Removing a saved session does not delete files you exported separately.

Click Export beside Copy in the floating toolbar to preview a PNG crop, choose a display, and select which measurements to include. Save the image to a location you choose or copy it to the clipboard; either action also retains the crop locally with the session. Review the preview for private content before sharing. Export Session saves measurement values and notes as JSON; images are saved individually from the session log.

Session titles accept up to 1,000 characters and notes up to 100,000. If pasted text exceeds the limit, the app keeps the accepted text and shows a message that the extra text was not added.

If a session cannot be saved or loaded, keep the error message and contact support. Unreadable library files are left untouched. For local storage locations and complete deletion options, see the privacy policy.

Shortcuts and customization

Settings lets you change tool order and visibility, palette style, units, detection defaults, loupe magnification, export padding, and keyboard shortcuts. Points are the default unit, and your selected unit is remembered. Choose Minimal or Detailed in the toolbar menu or under Settings → Workspace → Palette.

To change the interface language, open Settings → General → Language. Choose one of the 17 supported languages or Follow System, then click the Reopen Screen Ruler link to apply the change. Languages are listed by their native names. This changes Screen Ruler's language without changing your Mac's global language settings.

Tool shortcuts work while the measuring canvas has focus and do not run while you edit a text field. If the global shortcut conflicts with another app, change its key or modifiers in Settings → Shortcuts. Help → Screen Ruler Help and Settings → Privacy → Open help contain the in-app quick reference. The shortcut label follows your keyboard layout; the workspace and Help show your configured shortcut and whether it is disabled.

Open at login

Enable Open Screen Ruler at login under Settings → General. The switch reflects macOS’s current permission. If approval is needed, use Open Login Items settings to allow the app, or Cancel to remove the request. Opening at login does not start screen capture.

GET IN TOUCH

Still need help?

Email potatoplanet.studios@gmail.com with your Screen Ruler version, macOS version, display setup and scaling, the steps you took, and what you expected to happen. For capture problems, mention whether Playground works and whether the issue follows Freeze, Interact, or a display change.

Only include screenshots or session files if they help explain the issue, and remove private screen content, names, notes, and file paths first. We do not need your Mac password or Apple Account credentials.
